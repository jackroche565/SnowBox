# Snowbox

Snowfall and forecasts for Northeast US ski resorts. (First called Snowline; the repo is now `SnowBox`.) Next.js (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind v4), deployed on Vercel. The owner is new to coding: explain things plainly and keep changes focused.

## Deploying

Vercel auto-deploys the branch `claude/web-app-github-vercel-p9ocy4` as production. Pushing to it updates the live site (test123-iota-red.vercel.app). Other branches get Vercel preview URLs. Run `git pull` before starting work, since cloud and local sessions both push here.

## Before pushing

Run `npm run lint`, `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npm run build`. All must pass.

## Structure

- Each tab has one job. `/` Home: on a first visit, "Which mountains do you ski?" with states that expand to starred rows; after that, one headline number (most snow in the next 7 days, or the next snow) and the "Your week" grid: your mountains as rows, the next 7 days as columns, a bar per day sized by its snow, plus a total. Edit opens the picker. `/explore`: map-first on phones (Map/List text tabs in the header), list beside the map on desktop; tapping a dot opens a bottom panel with View mountain. Leads to `/resorts/[id]` (one page, no tabs, statically generated): spec strip, Snow (last 48 hrs, next 3 days, modeled base), Next 7 days as a bar chart with the days 8–16 total and plain sentences for the storm, refreeze, wind holds and opening date, hour by hour folded away, then links (official lifts & trails report first). `/decide`: one question, "where should I ski on this day?": a day strip, one answer (best bet, its snow and drive, View mountain) and three backups. With no snow that day it names the closest open mountain. Starting point and passes are asked once on a first visit, then sit in one "From Boston · Ikon · Change" line at the bottom. No ranking modes, drive limits or compare: keep it that simple. `/compare` redirects to `/decide` (in `next.config.ts`).
- Nothing unnecessary: people have limited time and attention. Before adding anything, ask whether it repeats something already on screen or makes someone stop and decode it; if so, leave it out. One entry point per action.
- Copy rules: the only title is "Snowbox" in the bar. No generated or changing headlines ("Storm Watch" and the like), no marketing sentences. Section labels are small uppercase labels, not big headings. State numbers plainly.
- Navigation: `src/components/Nav.tsx` holds the tabs: top tabs in the header from tablet width up, and a bottom tab bar on phones (the root layout pads the page for it).
- `src/data/resorts.json` holds all resort data for 76 areas: every public, lift-served downhill area in New England with at least 2 lifts or 300 ft of vertical, plus Hunter (NY). The original 19 have hand-checked stats and links. The other 57 came from OpenSkiMap (location, lift-served top and bottom elevation, all flagged as estimates) with homepages checked to load, stored as `websiteUrl`. Passes were checked against the Epic, Ikon and Indy sites in Sept 2026; they change yearly. The file holds coordinates, passes (`Epic`/`Ikon`/`Indy`), summit/base/vertical/trails, and official `trailMapUrl`/`snowReportUrl`/`webcamUrl`. `estimates` lists fields that are uncertain; the UI marks them "est.". `opensOn` is this season's projected opening day for 55 areas, from OnTheSnow's projected-openings pages (Oct 2026); the rest have no announced date. Refresh it every fall. `src/lib/season.ts` uses it: before opening day there are no wind holds, Decide ranks only open mountains, and the resort page shows the opening date in place of wind. Don't invent stats or URLs: look them up and flag anything unverified.
- `src/app/api/forecast/route.ts` fetches a light 16-day daily summary for all resorts from Open-Meteo in one request, cached 30 min. `src/lib/forecast.ts` parses it. Leave this data logic alone unless asked.
- `src/app/api/resort/[id]/route.ts` is the heavy per-resort forecast, fetched only on a resort page: 72 hours at summit and base elevation, and 16 days from three models (GFS, ECMWF, GEM) for a confidence range. `src/lib/resortForecast.ts` parses it and derives rain/snow type and wind holds. Keep heavy data here, not in the all-resort summary.
- Weather service (`src/lib/nws.ts`): free `api.weather.gov`, which asks every caller to send a User-Agent. `/api/alerts` fetches active alerts for our seven states once (cached 10 min) and matches them to each resort by its forecast zone or county, stored as `nws` in `resorts.json` (with the office, e.g. BTV). Only skier-relevant alerts (winter, wind, cold) are kept. Home shows one line per warning across your mountains; resort pages show the warning lines under the spec strip and the local office's forecaster discussion (key messages, else synopsis) folded away, via `/api/nws/[id]`.
- `src/lib/outlook.ts` makes Home's one-line note per mountain (powder day, next snow, flurries, or none). `src/lib/decide.ts` scores a resort for a day (weights at the top of the file) and estimates drive time from straight-line distance. Drive times are always labelled as estimates without traffic.
- `src/components/AppState.tsx` is shared state across pages: forecasts, favorites, the passes you hold, and your starting point (saved in localStorage under the older `snowline:` keys, kept so saved lists survive), plus Explore's sort. `PassPicker` and `LocationSearch` edit the shared passes and location from any page.
- `src/components/SiteHeader.tsx` is the same plain bar on every tab: Snowbox wordmark on the snow ground over a 1px ink rule, tabs from tablet width up, and an optional `aside` (Explore puts its Map/List tabs there). Terrain imagery sits below it, never behind it.
- The Explore map is MapLibre (`ResortMap.tsx`), loaded client-side only, on Snowbox's own style (`QUIET_STYLE` in `src/lib/terrain.ts`): snow land, ice-blue water, relief, dashed state lines, state and city names only. Flat and north-up. Dots are colored by snow (`SNOW_BUCKETS` in `src/lib/format.ts`): next 7 days, or observed in the last 48 hrs, with a layer switch and key across the top of the map; your mountains are slightly larger and labelled with name and total; the selected dot gets an ink ring. No zoom buttons or hover pop-ups. Layers are added on `style.load` (not `load`, which a background tab can hold back).
- Observed snow and radar: `/api/observed` reads NOAA's National Snowfall Analysis (NOHRSC, `src/lib/observed.ts`), the newest 00/12 UTC 24- and 48-hour GeoTIFFs (values in inches; the NetCDF version is meters), sampled at each resort's cell and checked hourly. The resort page's "Last 48 hrs" uses it when available (labelled as observed by NOAA, with the window's end), else the model. Radar is NOAA's national composite as tiles from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet (public domain, credited on the map), refreshed every 5 minutes. Explore's map switches between Next 7 days, Fell (48 hrs) and Radar.
- Live cams: `liveCams` in `resorts.json` lists YouTube live streams that the resort (or a partner it embeds, e.g. Mount Washington Observatory for Cranmore) publishes with embedding allowed; 8 cams at 6 resorts, checked Oct 2026. `/api/cams` re-checks them with YouTube's oEmbed every 6 hours so a restarted stream (new id) drops out instead of breaking. `LiveCam` shows YouTube's still until tapped, then plays muted in place. They show only on resort pages, under Snow; Home stays cam-free. Only embed cams whose owner allows it; other resorts' cams stay links. Mount Southington and Nashoba embed a channel live stream (only live in season), not yet added.
- Terrain: free, keyless sources in `src/lib/terrain.ts` (AWS Terrain Tiles elevation, OpenFreeMap basemap). Resort pages render a live 3D hero (`TerrainHero.tsx`, auto-aims at the high ground); Home uses a pre-rendered panorama of the Mount Mansfield range (`public/terrain/green-mountains.jpg`) whose sky is exactly the page color; re-render it the same way if it changes.
- Lifts and trails open: no free reliable source (Liftie blocks server requests; OnTheSnow's feed is paid), so resort pages link to the official report. Don't fake these numbers.

## Principles

- **No cards.** Drop the `sheet` surfaces (white, rounded, shadowed). Sections sit on the snow ground and are divided by rules: `1px solid #0f1a2a` between major sections, `1px solid #dfe5ec` between rows.
- **Blue means snow, nothing else.** Snow figures and bars use glacier `#1f5f8b` (darker than before), deep snow `#123f60`, light snow `#cfe1ee` / `#8fb7d4`, none `#cfd7e0`. Links use `#1f5f8b` too but only as text. Alpenglow `#e0532f` = powder day only. Barn red is no longer used for wind.
- **No pass colors.** Remove the Tailwind orange/blue/green dots everywhere (`PASS_COLORS`). Passes are text. On Home, a pass tag is filled ink when it's a pass the user holds (`myPasses`), outlined otherwise; resorts on no pass say "Independent".
- **Type does the work.** Archivo at narrow widths for anything that's a name or number: hero figures 92–104px at `font-stretch: 62%`, weight 800; names 17–19px at 75%, weight 700. Instrument Sans for reading text at 11/13/14/15px. Drop the "three sizes only" rule.
- **Controls are text, not chips.** Selected state = ink + bold + 2–3px ink underline. Filters read as a sentence with underlined tappable words.
- **Bottom tab bar:** text only (Home / Explore / Decide), Archivo 15px; active = weight 800 + 3px ink bar on top. No icons.
- **Wind hold:** wind line icon (stroke, ink) + the words "Wind hold possible". Never red, never an emoji.
- **Copy:** no captions that explain the UI ("Bars rise with…", "Red means…"). No fake timestamps. Sentence case. One plain sentence beats a styled box.
- **Header:** wordmark left, 1px ink rule under it.
